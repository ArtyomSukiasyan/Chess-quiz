Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy describes how Chess Quiz — the chess quiz game available on the web and as an Android app — handles your information. The short version: the quiz does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, sign-ups, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and the quiz does not use cookies.

Information stored on your device

The quiz saves two things in your browser's or the app's local storage so they are still there next time you play: your best score for each category and round length, and the language you chose (English, Armenian or Russian). These values never leave your device and are not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete them at any time by clearing the site data in your browser, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Your answers and score in the current round are kept only in memory and are lost when you close the quiz.

Hosting

The quiz is hosted on Vercel, and the Android app loads the quiz from there. Like any web host, Vercel may automatically process technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the page and protect against abuse. We do not access or use this information.

App permissions

The Android app uses only internet access, which it needs to load the quiz. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files, or any other personal data.

Children's privacy

Chess Quiz is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
